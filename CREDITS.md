 HEXKELL — CREDITS AND THIRD-PARTY ASSET LICENSES
================================================================================

Hexkell is an educational game for learning functional programming, developed
by Manuel Saavedra Soto as part of a memoria de título at the Facultad de
Ciencias Físicas y Matemáticas, Universidad de Chile.

All original code, the internal functional language and its interpreter, and
the procedurally drawn elements of the game (the hexes, their connections and
the grid) are the work of the author.
